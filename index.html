Child-consultation operations support

Public-scope sample for the Tochigi Prefecture (栃木県) notice on a generative-AI system for child consultation centres. Reiwa 8 (2026).

栃木県の公式システムではありません。入札書でも提案書でもありません。試作です。

No case work on this page. This sample does not search records, draft a response for a child, or keep what you type. It only restates the public notice and the linked specification. Do not enter a name, case number, school, family detail, or any other personal information.

What the public specification says

Figures and functions below are copied in substance from the prefecture’s public specification (仕様書), linked from the notice. They are not features of this file.

Named functions

  • Similar-case lookup (類似事例の調査): natural-language prompts over loaded child-consultation records, several matches ranked by relevance, each with a summary.
  • Draft directions for consideration (参考となる対応方針案の出力): several draft options for a specified case number, with cited statutes, notices, and guidelines.

The specification says summaries and drafts must carry record fields such as case number, family composition, and, for drafts, the child’s name and date of birth. This sample does not show those fields, because it has no records and will not invent any.

What it must not decide

  • The response direction itself.
  • Whether temporary protection is required (一時保護の要否).
  • Any other judgment that staff or the child consultation centre director must make.

The specification places the system as reference only. Final decisions stay with staff and the director, made organisationally.

Who, where, and how data is bounded

  • Child Policy Division (こども政策課): administration, and loading or updating records and statutes.
  • Central, Ken-nan, and Ken-hoku child consultation centres: similar-case lookup and consideration of a response direction.
  • Dedicated terminals, a closed network that does not cross the public internet, weekly full-replace CSV from the existing child-consultation system, domestic data centre, and no use of prompts, records, or outputs to train an external AI model.

Terminal counts stated in the specification: policy division 1 (about 5 people); central 20 (about 40 people, about 3,000 outputs a year); Ken-nan 17 (about 30 people, about 2,500); Ken-hoku 13 (about 20 people, about 1,300).
